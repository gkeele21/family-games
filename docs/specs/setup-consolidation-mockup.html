How rounds & scoring work: a round plays one question per team (2 teams → 2 questions). Each correct answer scores that round’s points/answer; sweep the whole board and the team also earns that round’s sweep bonus. Round 1 = 100/answer (+1,000 sweep), Round 2 = 200 (+2,000)… Questions pulled = rounds × teams.
