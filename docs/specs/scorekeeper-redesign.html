Keeler Games · Design spec

Scorekeeper redesign

Every screen re-skinned in the dark keeler-app palette. Identity is the Dashboard wordmark — white SCORE + red KEEPER — but red is logo-only; all actions follow the profile accent. Player identity is shown as colored score text, chosen from a curated neon palette (§02).

02

Player colors — pick-from library

The final set — 15 colors spread across the wheel (the 7 site tokens, tagged site, plus harmonious neon additions). Three unused hues are kept below in case you change your mind. Players show as colored score text, no cell background. As players are added they’re auto-assigned in a set order (previewed below, each color far from the last); anyone can override via the picker, and two players can’t share one in a game.
